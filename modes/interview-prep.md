# Mode: Interview Preparation

Generate company-specific interview preparation materials.

## Pre-flight

1. Read the evaluation report for this company/role
2. Read `cv.md` and `modes/_profile.md`
3. Read `interview-prep/story-bank.md` for existing stories

## Output

### 1. STAR+R Story Bank
Generate 6-10 stories using STAR+Reflection framework:
- **S**ituation: Context and challenge
- **T**ask: Your specific responsibility
- **A**ction: What you did (specific, technical)
- **R**esult: Measurable outcome
- **R**eflection: What you learned, what you'd do differently (signals seniority)

### 2. Company-Specific Intel
- Recent news, product launches, challenges
- Team structure and key people
- Interview process (from Glassdoor, Blind)
- Culture insights

### 3. Questions to Ask
- 5-7 thoughtful questions that demonstrate knowledge of the company
- Mix of role-specific, team, and strategic questions

### 4. Red Flag Responses
- Prepared answers for potential concerns (career gaps, short tenures, etc.)

## Save

- Append new stories to `interview-prep/story-bank.md`
- Save company report to `interview-prep/{company}-{role}.md`
