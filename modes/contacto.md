# Mode: LinkedIn Outreach

Find relevant contacts at a target company and draft personalized outreach messages.

## Pre-flight

1. Read `cv.md` for candidate background
2. Read `config/profile.yml` for target roles
3. Read `modes/_profile.md` for narrative framing

## Step 1 -- Find Contacts

Use WebSearch to find:
1. Hiring managers for the target role
2. Engineering leads / CTOs at the company
3. Recruiters working on the role
4. Mutual connections or alumni

## Step 2 -- Draft Messages

For each contact, draft a personalized LinkedIn message:

**Structure:**
1. Hook (1 sentence): Why you're reaching out to THIS person
2. Bridge (1-2 sentences): Relevant shared context or your fit
3. Ask (1 sentence): Specific, actionable request

**Tone:**
- Peer-to-peer, not applicant-to-gatekeeper
- Reference something specific about their work or the company
- Keep under 300 characters for connection requests, under 1000 for InMails

**Never:**
- Mass-message templates
- "I'm passionate about..."
- Ask for a referral in the first message
