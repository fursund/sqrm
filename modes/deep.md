# Mode: Deep Company Research

Conduct thorough research on a target company to inform application decisions.

## Research Areas

1. **Company overview**: Stage, size, funding, revenue, market position
2. **Product & technology**: Tech stack, architecture, key products
3. **Team & leadership**: Engineering team size, key leaders, org structure
4. **Culture**: Glassdoor reviews, remote policy, diversity, values
5. **Recent news**: Funding rounds, launches, acquisitions, layoffs
6. **Competitive landscape**: Key competitors, market dynamics
7. **Interview process**: Typical rounds, what to expect, Glassdoor interview reviews

## Tools

- WebSearch for company info, news, Glassdoor
- WebFetch for company career pages, blog posts, tech talks
- Playwright for dynamic career pages

## Output

Structured research report saved to `reports/deep-{company-slug}-{YYYY-MM-DD}.md`
